About me

I am currently a Postdoc in the Institute for Interdisciplinary Information Sciences (IIIS), Tsinghua University, where I am fortunate to be advised by Prof. Longbo Huang.

I received my Ph.D. in 2026 at Institute for Interdisciplinary Information Sciences (IIIS), Tsinghua University, advised by Prof. Longbo Huang, my B.E. in 2020 at Tsinghua University, major in Electronic Engineering and second major in Economics and Finance.

My research focuses on Reinforcement Learning (Theory, Algorithm and Application), Generative AI (Diffusion Models, Flow Models and Large Language Models) and Network Optimization.
